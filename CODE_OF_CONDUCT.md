# Code of Conduct

TreeWork contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, specific, and
respectful.

- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Provide evidence for bug reports and behavioral claims.
- Do not fabricate reproductions, test results, or project context.
- Respect contributor privacy and do not publish private project data.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive. Serious or private
concerns should be reported through the repository's private security advisory
channel rather than a public issue.
